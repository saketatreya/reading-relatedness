=== exp 051 — Phase 1: does the SHAPE channel carry descent beyond centroids? ===
VERDICT: NEAR-NULL (pre-registered null, reported as committed). Compute: jarvislabs L4.

SETUP. 36 languages / 6 families (FLORES-200 devtest, 1012 parallel sentences).
Per family: own-frame hub alignment (method/hub.py), held-out 200-sentence clouds.
A->B paired displacement D = X[b]-X[a] (meaning cancels by the pairing). Channels:
  centroid  d_cent  = ||mean_i D||          (the mean channel we always used)
  shape     d_shape = sqrt(tr cov_i D)      (validated hub-robust in exp 050)
Pre-registered PRIMARY: partial(shape, genealogy | centroid) > 0, perm-significant.

RESULT (n=94 within-family pairs):
                                     layer 8        layer 12
  corr(centroid, shape)             +0.801         +0.737     (channels 74-80% redundant)
  partial(centroid, gen | typ)      +0.138         +0.250     (033 replicates at L12: ~+0.27)
  partial(shape, gen | CENTROID)    +0.223         -0.196     <<PRIMARY>>
     permutation p                   0.049          0.113
  partial(shape, gen | cent, typ)   +0.187         -0.197

READ (honest). The shape channel does NOT robustly add descent beyond the centroid.
L8 gives a marginal +0.22 (p=.049 — does NOT survive correcting for 2 layers); L12,
the layer where the centroid best reproduces the established +0.27 descent signal,
the shape channel is NEGATIVE. The two channels are 0.74-0.80 correlated. So for the
DESCENT question, centroids are not badly lossy — shape magnitude is largely
redundant with the mean. This is the pre-registered null; we report it.

CAVEATS (temper the null, not excuses):
  - Single config (mean_pool, 2 layers, 200-sentence covariance) is noisier than the
    exp-033 ensemble (2 pools x 4 layers) that gave the robust +0.27.
  - This tested the CRUDEST shape summary: a scalar MAGNITUDE (sqrt tr cov). Descent
    could live in the transformation's DIRECTION (do sibling languages share
    displacement directions?), which the trace discards. That is a DISTINCT hypothesis
    (descent = transformation direction vs size), needs its own Phase-0 validation, and
    must equally commit to accepting a null. Not goalpost-moving; a different instrument.
  - Clock channel (shape magnitude vs divergence TIME) was not tested here.

WHAT STANDS. The centroid descent signal and the molecular clock (prior work) are
untouched — the broad thesis (SSL geometry encodes the process) does not depend on
this. What deflates is the strong "centroids are EXTREMELY lossy" claim, for descent.

PROVENANCE. experiment.py (self-contained, clones rep-phylogeny, extracts XLM-R-large,
FLORES-200). run_output.txt = the raw job SUMMARY. pairs.csv was lost (orchestrator
downloaded /root/out but the box home was /home; SUMMARY was printed to the log). A
re-run for per-family/per-language detail needs the download path fixed + pairs.csv
echoed to stdout.
